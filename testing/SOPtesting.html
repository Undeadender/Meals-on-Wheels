Security SOP

General

The handbrake can be used to turn the FRV quickly.
It is more important to be in control of the FRV than it is to be fast.
Flipping over or ejecting teammates slows you way down.
Try to keep the map visible to be able to orient yourself.
DO NOT try to be your own Navigator.
Drive is the main gear you use as it allows you to reach greater speeds
1st and 2nd gear should not be ignored.
The limited speed can be beneficial, especially on difficult terrain.

Parking

Whenever parking the car make it clear you are stopping by saying, “DISMOUNT”. Avoid Parking near barrels or in open areas, alleys often work well If the FRV is damaged or overrun after parking, it is better to find a safe place and drop a new one Park in a position where the HMG turret is useful if possible, examples below.

Hazards

Keep these in mind when driving the FRV

Illuminate barriers
Can contain Hidden barrels that can be driven through w/o blowing barrels, but Illuminate can hit a barrel causing it to explode

Destroyed Cars
Solid but driving over them will cause them to explode

Illuminate batteries
These will explode during collisions, killing passengers

Illuminate Beacon
Can be driven through at moderate speeds, but are solid at low speeds

Sand bags
Solid, no matter the speed

Harvester
Beam can melt the FRV, and pop a tire

Fleshmob
Attacks can Flip FRV
Acts as a solid barrier

Stingray
Can heavily damage the FRV in a single strafe

Leviathan
Can cause extreme damage to the FRV

Illuminate Obstacles
Lighting Spire can wipe the team while, the Gazer can heavily damage the FRV. Barrels do heavy damage and set Helldivers alight.
Are best destroyed by Spotter with the GL

Armor
Longevity in the fight and ability to quickly engage is paramount. Peak Physique armor passive allows for rapid acquisition with long guns and AC, better melee keeps you in the fight longer. Reinforced Epaulettes has similar melee damage and faster reloading, and thus gives more uptime on primary.

Tactical Info
AC Flak Targets: Fleshmob, Stingray, Voteless Horde, Elevated Overseer, Watcher, Outpost Shields
AC APHET Targets: Harvester (leg joint), Overseer (head), Lightning Spire, Tesla Tower, Outpost Doors
Never let your Autocannon go completely empty. Always try to reload after firing 5 rounds. This will speed up your reloads greatly and keep you in the fight.

FRV - During loadout always call in your FRV first. Never call in your FRV if the team is expecting to stay in the area. Don't let your FRV be collateral in your bloodthirst for the Illuminate.
Tesla Tower - The Tesla Tower can wipe an ENTIRE TEAM in an instant if misplaced. DO NOT place a Tesla Tower within 50m of squad. It will zone as well, if not better, than a turret if placed properly. The Benefit over a turret is it will not stray fire into the Whaling Team or their Shield Generator Relays.
