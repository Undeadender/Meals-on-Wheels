Armor
Armor for Gunner is chosen solely for survivability. You MUST run the heaviest armor, best for this is the CPR-80 Bulwark. It will keep the Gunner alive during takedowns, inside Stingray strafes, and on the FRV turret. Heavy Fortified is best if the CPR-80 Bulwark is unavalable. Heavy extra padded can also be effective.

FLEX STRATAGEMS
Tesla Tower - Deploy tower 50m from the squad. This is the MoW alternative to a turret. It does not affect the shield relay and if used correctly, will not harm the Whaling Team.
Gas/EMS mortar - Gas is preffered, deploy at takedown sites and objectives.
Orbital Laser - For destroying outposts efficiently in a blitz.
Portable Hellbomb - For destroying Monoliths on the Free the City mission. Do not call in your Shield Generator Pack or Recoilless until after the Monolith is destroyed. Spotter will assist in delivering the Hellbomb.
